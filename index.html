🛠️ Microcontroller Firmware & Wiring

Arduino C++ Sketch with Firebase-ESP-Client
ESP32 Multi-Sensor (MQ-135 + DHT22) Firmware

Recommended Pin Connections:

  • MQ-135 Gas Sensor: VCC → 5V (or Vin), GND → GND, AOUT → A0 (ESP8266) or GPIO 34 (ESP32 ADC1).
  • DHT22 / DHT11 (Temp & Humidity): VCC → 3.3V, GND → GND, DATA → D4 / GPIO 4 (with 10k pullup).
  • PMS5003 / PMS7003 (Optional Laser Dust): TX → SoftwareSerial RX (GPIO 16).
💡 Tip: For the MQ-135, allow a 24-48 hour initial burn-in period to achieve maximum sensor stability and accurate baseline calibration.
